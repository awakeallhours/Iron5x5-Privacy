# Privacy Policy & Data Disclosure for Iron 5x5

Effective Date: November 2026  
Developer: awakeallhours  
Contact Email: support@awakeallhours.com

## 1. Overview
**Iron 5x5** is built on a simple principle: **your training data belongs strictly to you.** The app operates entirely offline on your device. We do not require you to create an account, and we do not collect, transmit, sell, or store any of your personal or workout data on external servers.

## 2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device
All information you enter into Iron 5x5 is stored exclusively in a local database (`SQLite`) and local app preferences on your device's internal storage. This includes:

* **Workout Logs:** Exercise names, weights, sets, repetitions, dates, and workout notes.
* **App Preferences:** Your chosen unit system (`kg` or `lbs`), rest timer settings, barbell weights, and health disclaimer acknowledgment status.
* **Purchase Status:** A local record confirming whether you have unlocked the Lifetime Pass.

Because this data never leaves your device, **we have zero access to it.**

## 3. Data Sharing, Analytics, and Tracking
* **No Analytics or Telemetry:** Iron 5x5 contains no third-party analytics SDKs, crash-reporting trackers, or behavioral monitoring tools.
* **No Advertising:** The app contains no advertisements and does not share device identifiers with ad networks.
* **No Cloud Sync:** We do not run cloud servers. If you uninstall the app or clear its storage without making a backup, your local workout data will be permanently deleted by your device's operating system.

## 4. User-Initiated Backups and Exports
Iron 5x5 includes built-in tools allowing you to export your workout history as `.json` backup files or `.csv` spreadsheets using your device's native share sheet.

* Exporting data is **100% manual and initiated solely by you**.
* Once you export a file to your device storage, email, or a third-party cloud drive (such as Google Drive or iCloud), that file is governed by the privacy policy of the destination service you chose.

## 5. In-App Purchases
Iron 5x5 offers a one-time **Lifetime Pass** processed securely through the **Apple App Store** or **Google Play Store**.

* We do not receive, process, or store your credit card details, billing address, or payment credentials.
* All billing transactions and purchase restorations are handled directly by Apple or Google under their respective privacy policies.

## 6. Device Permissions
Iron 5x5 only requests minimal system capabilities required for core functionality:

* **Local Notifications / Audio / Vibration:** Used solely to alert you when your between-set rest timer finishes.
* **File Access (On-Demand):** Used only when you explicitly choose to import or export a workout backup file.

## 7. Health & Fitness Disclaimer
Iron 5x5 is a workout logging and progression tracking tool, **not a medical device or medical service**.

* Always consult a qualified healthcare professional before starting any new strength training or exercise program.
* You assume full responsibility for your physical safety, proper lifting form, and equipment setup while using this app.

## 8. Children's Privacy
Iron 5x5 does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the UK/EEA).
